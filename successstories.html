Success Stories

Aarti Sharma - Reviving Handloom

Arti Sharma from Varanasi revived her family’s traditional handloom weaving by launching a small online store. With growing support and visibility, she now employs 15 local weavers and exports her sarees to international markets.

Meena Patel - Clay Wonders

Meena Patel turned her childhood passion for clay modeling into 'Clay Wonders,' a homegrown brand of eco-friendly decor. Her handcrafted pieces gained popularity through social media, allowing her to train rural women and expand her business across India.

Ramesh Verma - Carving Success

Ramesh Verma, a traditional woodcarver from Saharanpur, once struggled to find buyers and barely made ends meet. Now his art found recognition nationwide—he now runs a successful business and trains aspiring artisans across India.
