What materials do I need for the workshop? ▶
You will need basic painting supplies like acrylic paints, brushes, and canvas. A full list will be shared upon registration.

Nope! This workshop is beginner-friendly and suitable for all age groups.
Will I get a certificate? ▶
Yes, all participants will receive a certificate of participation after the session.
