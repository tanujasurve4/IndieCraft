# 🎨 IndieCraft – Platform for Craft Makers

A web-based platform that helps **craft makers and artisans showcase, promote, and sell their handmade products online**.

IndieCraft provides a simple digital marketplace where craft makers can display their products and customers can explore and purchase unique handmade crafts.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Many local craft makers and artisans face difficulties in reaching customers and selling their handmade products online.

**IndieCraft** provides a platform that connects craft makers with customers and helps promote traditional and handmade products through an online marketplace.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- 🎨 Provide an online platform for craft makers.
- 🛍️ Allow artisans to showcase their handmade products.
- 👥 Connect craft makers with customers.
- 📢 Help promote local and traditional crafts.
- 💰 Support craft makers in increasing their sales.
- 🌐 Provide an easy-to-use online marketplace.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 👤 User Registration & Login
- 🎨 Craft Maker Registration
- 🛍️ Product Listing
- 📸 Product Images
- 🔍 Search & Browse Crafts
- 🛒 Add to Cart
- 💳 Order / Purchase Management
- 📦 Order Tracking
- ❤️ Product Wishlist
- ⭐ Product Reviews & Ratings
- 👨‍🎨 Craft Maker Profile
- 📊 Product & Order Management
